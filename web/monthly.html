How percentage sizing works

Each scenario sizes every entry from the simulated account's current equity: risk budget = equity × risk %; planned loss per lot = (|entry − stop| + slippage) × 100 oz + round-trip commission; volume rounded down to the 0.01 step.

A losing streak shrinks the budget, and a run of losses at 10 % can cut simulated equity substantially. Leverage 1:500 is used only for the margin check. These settings are never applied to MT5, Vantage, the live paper monitor or any account.

How a research study works

Every strategy (A, B, C and R1–R9) is studied over every stored month. The originals are kept unchanged as benchmarks. Each original gets exit experiments (its entry unchanged, one exit dimension changed) and a redesigned research version chosen from a small pre-declared set of variants (Example A v3, B v3, C v5, R1–R9 v2).

Variants are chosen on the development months only; walk-forward folds choose on earlier months and test on the next; the hold-out months are evaluated once. Expectancy is in R after costs per trade; every month is a separate $1,000 account. Research versions never change a saved strategy, a monitor or an account.
