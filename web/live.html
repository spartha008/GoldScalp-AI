MT5 demo-account execution

When a monitored version is linked here, each of its SIMULATED validation trades is also sent as a real order, with the same side, volume, stop-loss and take-profit, to the MT5 account your terminal is logged in to. Only an account that reports itself as a DEMO account is accepted, checked before every order. The order is closed when the simulated trade closes. It is never sent more than the configured delay after the simulated entry, and never resent.
