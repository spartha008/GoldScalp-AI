Project overview · updated 16 November 2026

Every phase of GoldScalp AI, in one place

GoldScalp AI is an educational XAUUSD research and paper-trading application. Between 13 and 15 November 2026 it grew from live market data and a lot-based paper simulator into market-intelligence analysis, a Strategy Lab, live strategy validation on the Vantage MT5 demo feed, and a separate monthly research service. The research found no strategy edge that held up out of sample: Phase 6 closed on 15 November 2026 and strategy discovery is paused. Since then Phases 1–6 have been archived read-only, forward data has been locked in code and published gold-scalping ideas have been screened. One screened research candidate, P7-FIX, is observed on the MT5 demo account at the owner's request, and the Dashboard gained a manual order ticket for that demo account. Phase 7 has not started.

Ground rules that apply to every phase

Simulation firstEvery strategy trade is SIMULATED in the paper simulator. No real-money order path exists. The only order path is optional, off by default (switched on in this installation since 15 November 2026) and limited to an MT5 demo account; it carries the strategy mirror and, since 16 November 2026, a manual order ticket.
MT5 is read-only for dataMarket data comes through an allowlist of read functions. GoldScalp never asks for, stores or logs the MT5 login or password, and never starts the terminal.
Research is isolatedThe research service reads the application database read-only, writes only to its own store and never connects to MT5.
Data is never inventedMissing, invalid or duplicate candles are reported, never filled. Months without enough data are marked INCOMPLETE.
Criteria frozen before resultsEvaluation rules are hash-frozen before the data they judge exists, and failed experiments are kept, not hidden.
Forward data lockedSince 15 November 2026 research code cannot read candles from 14 November 2026 onward. No setting turns this off; only a registered, versioned unlock protocol could.
Local and transparentThe app binds to 127.0.0.1, providers never switch silently, and every figure is labelled current, historical or simulated.

Feature

MT5 demo-account execution (optional)

14 Nov 2026on · 1 version linked

An unnumbered addition, documented in the README. When switched on and linked, it mirrors the SIMULATED validation trades of a linked strategy version as market orders on an MT5 demo account so broker fills can be compared.

Safeguards

  • The account is re-read before every order; anything that is not the linked DEMO account is refused, and a real-money account disables every link.
  • Limits: 3 open demo positions, no late entries, no retries; a "Stop all demo execution" kill switch. The default is 0.10 lots per order; this installation allows 2.00 lots since 16 November 2026 (P7-FIX on demo).
  • A trade without a stop-loss is never sent.
  • Disabled unless GOLDSCALP_DEMO_EXECUTION_ENABLED is set. The owner switched it on on 15 November 2026, with no version linked. On 16 November P7-FIX v2 was linked first (12:27 UTC); since 13:03 UTC the only linked version is P7-FIX v3, a research candidate at the owner's 5% risk per trade (1× ATR stop, 2R target, break-even at 1R).

Phase 6

New strategy discovery

15 Nov 2026concluded

A new method, not a new round of tuning: three genuinely different hypotheses, each written down before any code ran, tested on development data, and validated once on a sealed period nothing has touched.

The questions

  • H6.1: does the volatility regime decide whether trend continuation or a liquidity-sweep reversal works?
  • H6.2: does a fresh H1 change of character add information beyond a plain H1 trend?
  • H6.3: do session-level breaks around the London open keep going while Asian-session breaks fail, month after month?

The rules

Development: January–13 November 2026 only. Sealed: November 2023 – October 2025 (24 months), unreadable by development code. Forward data from 14 November 2026 is kept for confirmation. At most four trials per hypothesis, refinements built by the tool with a written rationale, and rejection accepted.

On development data

  • H6.1 was not supported as predicted, H6.2 was rejected (the extra structure made results worse) and H6.3 was inconclusive (too few London-open trades). None went to the sealed data.
  • Plain trend continuation (VOL-C) looked strong: +0.102 R per trade over 294 trades, 9 of 11 months positive.

The one sealed test

H6.4 rejected. VOL-C outside contracting M15 volatility, registered after development with its origin disclosed, ran once on November 2023 – October 2025. −0.006 R per trade over 663 trades, 11 of 24 months positive, negative with doubled costs and on M15. The unfiltered VOL-C made −0.029 R per trade: the development edge did not carry over. Nothing is retuned or promoted.

With H6.4 rejected, Phase 6 closed on 15 November 2026 (Phase 6 closed). Every Phase 5 and Phase 6 study can be compared side by side, with charts, in the Research library (Monthly Backtest, step 5).

Closure

Phase 6 closed, strategy discovery paused, research library

15 Nov 2026closed

H6.4 was the only Phase 6 hypothesis to reach the sealed data, and it was rejected, so Phase 6 closed on 15 November 2026 with nothing retuned or promoted. The owner then paused strategy discovery: no new variants, hypotheses or sealed tests until they set the next direction, with the focus on better evidence rather than more strategies.

Research library (Monthly Backtest, step 5)

  • Lists every Phase 5 and Phase 6 study, read-only, from the research store and the frozen report files.
  • Compares up to eight results side by side: pooled metrics, cumulative R by month, a monthly R heatmap, costs ×1 / ×2 / ×3 and, for Phase 6, regime breakdowns.
  • Follows one strategy across every study and opens the written reports in a built-in reader. Classes are shown as each study recorded them, never recomputed; incomplete and failed studies stay listed.
  • On 16 November it also gained the pre-Phase 7 screens (filter “Pre-7 · Screens”).

What closing means

The sealed November 2023 – October 2025 block is spent: it can no longer serve as unseen data for any idea shaped by this result. The Phase 6 discovery protocol and the H6.4 protocol stay frozen by hash and report unchanged.

Guard

Forward-data guard in code

15 Nov 2026locked

Research can no longer read candles from 14 November 2026 00:00:00 UTC onward, so the only data research has not used stays out of reach until a frozen protocol unlocks it. Finished at 19:03 UTC, before the market reopened at 23:00 UTC.

How it works

  • One module (goldscalp/core/forward.py, definition forward-guard@1) checks every research request before any data is read, and every candle frame after it is read, including look-back history and outcome candles.
  • A request that touches the forward period is refused with HTTP 409 and its reason; nothing is truncated. This covers the Strategy Lab, Performance, improvement studies and every research-service job.
  • No setting, environment variable or flag turns it off. Unlocking needs a versioned forward-unlock@1 protocol registered with its SHA-256; none is registered, and no endpoint accepts one yet.
  • Not guarded, on purpose: live MT5 collection, charts and market endpoints, the dashboard, replay, the live monitor and demo execution.

Checked

17 new tests. After both services restarted (19:01 and 19:02 UTC), a Strategy Lab backtest to 16 November, a Performance run to 20 November and a research request to 1 December were refused with 409, and no run was created. Pre-7

External strategy research and screening

15–16 Nov 2026screening only

Before any Phase 7: a search for publicly documented gold scalping ideas, each screened on development data that research had already used. A short screen is not validation, and nothing was promoted.

What was done

  • 14 documented concepts collected from papers, books, broker education and vendor posts, with their rules, evidence and limits recorded.
  • Stage 1 (15 November): the 3 concepts the strategy builder could express, screened on 5–19 October 2026 (10 trading days). All 3 inconclusive; none qualified as worth further research.
  • Stage 2 (16 November): new research tools (an indicator library and a rule simulator with clock-time entries, stop orders and reversals, using the backtester's fills and costs), then 15 rule sets covering every remaining concept, on the same 10 trading days plus an 11-week context run (17 August – 31 October).
  • Tool checks: 436 of 436 backtester trades replayed with the same exit and net result, and no look-ahead. The screen's loader refuses the sealed block and forward data; every read ended by 31 October 2026.

What came out

No strategy is validated. On the 10-day screen 2 rule sets passed, 8 had too few trades and 5 were rejected. Over 11 weeks the “short gold before the London fixes” rule held up (+0.20 R per trade over 110 trades; its two half hours ranked 1st and 2nd of 46 in a placebo test) and the gold London-breakout rule did well (+0.26 R over 52 trades), while the time-of-day rule that passed the screen fell to zero. All of it is spent development data from a market that fell about 11 %; any real test needs untouched data under a frozen protocol. The stage 2 report's shortlist for Phase 7, none of it started: P7-FIX, P7-ARBG (London breakout), P7-HALF (last-half-hour momentum) and P7-PCTB (Bollinger %b, only with a stop).

The two rule sets that passed the screen became research candidates (Candidates). Reports: PRE_PHASE_7_EXTERNAL_STRATEGY_RESEARCH_REPORT.md and PRE_PHASE_7_STAGE_2_SCREENING_REPORT.md.

Dashboard

MT5 demo order ticket

16 Nov 2026demo only

A panel under the Dashboard's candle chart for the owner's own market orders on the MT5 demo account (virtual funds). It is a manual tool, separate from every strategy; its orders are not research and not evidence for any strategy.

How it works

  • Choose Buy or Sell and the lots. The stop-loss and take-profit are lines on the candle chart: drag them, or type the prices.
  • Before sending, each line's label shows the USD result and the R multiple at that price, and the ticket shows the risk as a share of the balance. The spread is included; the broker's commission and swap are not.
  • “Review order” opens a confirmation dialog, and only “Send … to MT5 demo” in that dialog sends the order.
  • After sending, the position's entry, SL and TP lines stay on the chart with its live P/L. Its SL and TP can be dragged and applied, and it can be closed at market.

Safeguards

The demo-only gateway re-reads the account before every order and refuses anything that is not the DEMO account shown on the ticket. A stop-loss is required, the 2.00-lot limit applies, and demo execution and MT5's Algo Trading must both be on. Kept apart from the strategy mirror: ticket orders carry magic number 26111401 and the comment GoldScalp manual M<id> (the mirror uses 26111400). The mirror and the kill switch never touch ticket positions, and the ticket can change or close only its own. Every request, result and refusal is recorded (manual_orders, migration 0008). 15 tests run against a fake broker, so no test sends a real order.

It was first checked in the browser with the dialog cancelled. The owner placed the first ticket order at 13:23 UTC on 16 November: a 0.10-lot sell with a stop-loss and take-profit, which the owner closed at market at 13:26 UTC from the same ticket. Code: goldscalp/execution/manual.py, goldscalp/api/routers/manual.py and web/js/ticket.js; README section “Dashboard order ticket (MT5 demo account)”.
