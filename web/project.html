Project overview · updated 15 November 2026

Every phase of GoldScalp AI, in one place

GoldScalp AI is an educational XAUUSD research and paper-trading application. Between 13 and 15 November 2026 it grew from live market data and a lot-based paper simulator into market-intelligence analysis, a Strategy Lab, live strategy validation on the Vantage MT5 demo feed, and a separate monthly research service. The research found no strategy edge that held up out of sample, and the strategy search is now closed.

Ground rules that applied to every phase

Simulation firstEvery strategy trade is SIMULATED in the paper simulator. No real-money order path exists; the only order feature is optional, off by default and limited to an MT5 demo account.
MT5 is read-only for dataMarket data comes through an allowlist of read functions. GoldScalp never asks for, stores or logs the MT5 login or password, and never starts the terminal.
Research is isolatedThe research service reads the application database read-only, writes only to its own store and never connects to MT5.
Data is never inventedMissing, invalid or duplicate candles are reported, never filled. Months without enough data are marked INCOMPLETE.
Criteria frozen before resultsEvaluation rules are hash-frozen before the data they judge exists, and failed experiments are kept, not hidden.
Local and transparentThe app binds to 127.0.0.1, providers never switch silently, and every figure is labelled current, historical or simulated.

Phase 1

Live data, lot-based paper trading and the dashboard

13 Nov 2026implemented

The foundation: market data with explicit LIVE / DELAYED / HISTORICAL / DISCONNECTED states and a paper simulator that trades in lots.

What it built

  • Core settings, UTC clock, timeframes, trading schedule, and SQLite (WAL) with numbered migrations.
  • Data providers: gold-api.com (verified live) and Tiingo (built, not verified live), plus CSV import for generic, MT5-export and HistData files with validation and gap reports.
  • Lot-based paper simulator: market orders with stop-loss and take-profit, spread, commission, slippage, margin, and a Decimal balance ledger.
  • The dashboard and Paper Trading pages, basic structure / sessions / volatility analysis, and an optional display-only TradingView chart.

Result

167 tests; all 4 planted faults caught by mutation checks.
Phase 1.1

MetaTrader 5 demo data (read-only)

13 Nov 2026verified live

Prices and candles from the user's logged-in Vantage MT5 demo terminal, read through an allowlist that makes trading and account functions unreachable.

What it built

  • MT5 provider on a dedicated thread: never launches the terminal, passes no credentials, ranks gold symbols and maps server time to the New York-close rule.
  • Provider hub with priority, user selection and manual fallback by default; every source change is logged and shown.
  • Dashboard provider selector, Reconnect button and an MT5 details panel; CSRF header on every state-changing call.

Result

Verified with the market open: terminal build 6317, LIVE ticks 0.4–0.5 s old, spread 0.28–0.29; 0 rejected or duplicate bars; 231 tests.
Phase 2

Smart Market Intelligence

14 Nov 2026implemented

Deterministic market analysis that only ever uses candles closed at the time being analysed, plus replay and historical evaluation.

What it built

  • Inspection first: found and fixed a look-ahead defect where derived higher-timeframe candles were marked closed too early.
  • Versioned engines for structure (BOS / CHoCH), liquidity and sweeps, fair value gaps, order-block candidates, multi-timeframe state, sessions (DST-correct) and volatility.
  • Signal Centre: bullish / bearish / neutral / no signal with gates, evidence and an evidence agreement score that is explicitly not a probability.
  • New Replay and Performance pages, and per-timeframe chart annotations.

Result

372 tests; 21 planted defects caught. A historical signal evaluation ran on all recorded MT5 data (816 steps). No profitability figures were claimed.
Phase 3

Strategy Lab: strategy intelligence and advanced backtesting

14 Nov 2026complete

Versioned rule-based strategies and a look-ahead-free backtester, with walk-forward testing and comparisons.

What it built

  • Strategy Builder: 12 condition types, versions with logic fingerprints, JSON import / export, and example strategies A, B and C (examples, not claims of profitability).
  • Backtester: next-candle-open fills, spread / commission / slippage / margin, structure, ATR or fixed stops, and stop-first on ambiguous candles; dataset and result hashes.
  • Metrics with ratios withheld below 30 trades, walk-forward folds (rolling or anchored), a comparison lab, saved reports and exports.

Results (29 Oct – 13 Nov, M5)

Example C: 53 trades, +80.68 USD, PF 1.23, +0.014 R. Example A: 37 trades, +49.63 USD, PF 1.13, −0.029 R. Example B: 2 trades (too few). Walk-forward: in-sample +106 USD against −56 USD out of sample; 2 of 5 folds positive. 444 tests.
Phase 4

Live strategy validation, MT5 verification and research assistant

14 Nov 2026archived

The example strategies are watched on the live Vantage demo feed and traded in SIMULATED validation accounts, with a journal and per-trade evaluations.

What it built

  • Vantage MT5 verification (a 14-check record), candle integrity checks and feed health.
  • Live strategy monitor using the backtest's own as-of logic; one SIMULATED position per qualifying signal in each version's own validation account.
  • Journal with CSV export and a permanent seven-question evaluation of every trade.
  • Improvement engine (discovery, embargo and unseen hold-out windows) where a change is adopted only when the user approves it.
  • Research assistant with read-only tools; the optional Claude mode is off by default. All of it on the Live Validation page.

Status

Monitoring A v2, B v2 and C v4 since 14 Nov, 11:57 UTC (paper mode, active). Terminal record PARTIAL (11 pass, 3 warn, 0 fail); a fresh-tick VERIFIED run is pending the market open. C v4 improvement proposal: inconclusive, not adopted. 505 tests.
Feature

MT5 demo-account execution (optional)

14 Nov 2026off by default

An unnumbered addition, documented in the README. When switched on and linked, it mirrors the SIMULATED validation trades of a linked strategy version as market orders on an MT5 demo account so broker fills can be compared.

Safeguards

  • The account is re-read before every order; anything that is not the linked DEMO account is refused, and a real-money account disables every link.
  • Limits: 0.10 lots per order, 3 open demo positions, no late entries, no retries; a "Stop all demo execution" kill switch.
  • Disabled unless GOLDSCALP_DEMO_EXECUTION_ENABLED is set; it is not enabled in this installation.

Note

Some older documents still say no order path exists at all. That remains true for real-money accounts; this demo-only feature is the one exception.
Phase 5

Monthly multi-timeframe research and backtesting

14 Nov 2026built and tested

A separate research service (port 8001) that evaluates the exact strategy versions on a single month, each run in its own hypothetical $1,000 account.

What it built

  • Isolated service with its own store; reads the application database read-only and never connects to MT5.
  • Nine runs per month (A v2, B v2, C v4 on M15, M5 and M1) with 1:500 leverage used for margin only.
  • Research candidates R1–R9 developed strictly on data before the month; PDF, CSV and JSON reports with exact reproduction.
  • Repaired an MT5 time-basis incident that had re-dated stored candles (2,068 of 2,068 bars restored), with one user-approved restart.

Result (October 2026)

A, B and C lost on every completed run, for example A on M15 −22.7 % and C on M15 −24.1 %. M1 was INCOMPLETE (no M1 data yet). 534 tests.
Phase 5.1

M1 history import and percentage-risk scenarios

14 Nov 2026done

Real October M1 history from the user's MT5 export, and hypothetical stress tests that size every entry from current equity.

What it built

  • Historical import with a store-nothing preview, idempotent imports and per-month coverage checks (October M1: 99.8 % coverage).
  • Percentage-of-equity sizing at 5 %, 7.5 % and 10 %, each in its own $1,000 account: 27 runs.

Result

All 27 risk-scenario runs lost money, and higher risk magnified the losses (A at 10 % ended at $245.66, −75.4 %). For these rules M1 matched M5: it adds execution detail, not independent evidence.
Phase 5.2

Strategy redesign and multi-month robustness

14–15 Nov 2026completed

Why the originals lose, then redesigned research versions tested across June–November with walk-forward folds and a November hold-out. Criteria were frozen before any hold-out result existed.

What it built

  • Diagnostics of the 12 originals, with exit experiments changing one exit dimension at a time.
  • Redesigned versions A v3, B v3, C v5 and R1–R9 v2 chosen on development months only; 565 configurations in the full study.
  • Optional break-even and trailing stops, and a frozen verdict framework (promising / inconclusive / rejected / overfit).

Result

Promising: R2 v2 only (+0.054 R/trade out of sample), a thin edge that depended on October being secondary. Inconclusive: B v3, R1 v2, R4 v2, R5 v2, R7 v2. Rejected: A v3, C v5, R3 v2, R6 v2, R8 v2, R9 v2. Break-even and trailing stops made no measurable difference.
Phase 5.3

Frozen pre-June historical validation

15 Nov 2026completed

The Phase 5.2 shortlist, locked unchanged, run on January–May 2026 history it had never seen. The protocol was frozen while that history did not yet exist in the store, and nothing was selected.

What it built

  • A versioned, hash-frozen protocol with explicit five-month rules (at least 4 of 5 months).
  • Four locked candidates plus their originals, on M5 and M15, with costs at baseline, ×2 and ×3: 80 runs.

Result

None held up. R2 v2 −0.054 R/trade (273 trades), R5 v2 −0.053, R7 v2 −0.107: rejected. R1 v2 +0.006 (about break-even): inconclusive. Costs were not the cause (0.012–0.025 R per trade).
Closure

Phase 5 strategy search closed

15 Nov 2026closed

Phase 5.2 found an apparently promising candidate; independent validation showed it did not generalize. Rather than tune again, the cycle was closed.

  • Phase 5.3 frozen by hash: report, protocol, results and classifications cannot change unnoticed; no further validation run is accepted.
  • All four candidates retired from promotion; none goes to paper-monitor observation. Phase 4 continues unchanged.
  • Every candle stored so far (2 Nov 2025 – 13 Nov 2026) counts as used: a new idea must be tested on fresh data.

Phase 6

New strategy discovery

15 Nov 2026concluded

A new method, not a new round of tuning: three genuinely different hypotheses, each written down before any code ran, tested on development data, and validated once on a sealed period nothing has touched.

The questions

  • H6.1: does the volatility regime decide whether trend continuation or a liquidity-sweep reversal works?
  • H6.2: does a fresh H1 change of character add information beyond a plain H1 trend?
  • H6.3: do session-level breaks around the London open keep going while Asian-session breaks fail, month after month?

The rules

Development: January–13 November 2026 only. Sealed: November 2023 – October 2025 (24 months), unreadable by development code. Forward data from 14 November 2026 is kept for confirmation. At most four trials per hypothesis, refinements built by the tool with a written rationale, and rejection accepted.

On development data

  • H6.1 was not supported as predicted, H6.2 was rejected (the extra structure made results worse) and H6.3 was inconclusive (too few London-open trades). None went to the sealed data.
  • Plain trend continuation (VOL-C) looked strong: +0.102 R per trade over 294 trades, 9 of 11 months positive.

The one sealed test

H6.4 rejected. VOL-C outside contracting M15 volatility, registered after development with its origin disclosed, ran once on November 2023 – October 2025. −0.006 R per trade over 663 trades, 11 of 24 months positive, negative with doubled costs and on M15. The unfiltered VOL-C made −0.029 R per trade: the development edge did not carry over. Nothing is retuned or promoted.

Every Phase 5 and Phase 6 study can now be compared side by side, with charts, in the Research library (Monthly Backtest, step 5).
